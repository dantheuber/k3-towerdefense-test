ACHIEVEMENTS

FIELD MANUAL

OBJECTIVE

Enemies march along the glowing path toward your Core. Build towers to destroy them before they arrive. Each leak costs lives — lose them all and the Core falls.

ECONOMY

Kills and wave bonuses earn gold. At the end of every wave you earn interest on your gold reserves — saving is a strategy. Calling the next wave early grants bonus gold — but waves overlap, so greed has a price.

META PROGRESSION

Every run — win or lose — earns ⬢ cores and XP. Spend cores in the Tech Tree on permanent upgrades and new towers. Win maps to unlock new operations and harder difficulties with bigger rewards. Achievements grant permanent buffs.

TACTICS

• Armor (grey plating) reduces physical damage — poison ignores it.
• Shields (cyan) must be broken before HP takes damage.
• Splitters burst into swarmlings — bring splash.
• Juggernauts (wave 16+) are walking fortresses. Wraiths (wave 18+) ignore slows and freezes.
• On Hard: Menders heal nearby allies — kill them first. Phase Stalkers blink forward past your lines.
• On Nightmare: Broodmothers birth swarmlings as they walk.
• Bosses arrive every 10 waves, with escorts on higher difficulties.
• Selling refunds part of a tower's total investment.
• The Amplifier boosts adjacent towers — cluster your killers.
